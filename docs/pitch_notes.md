# Pitch / Demo Script

## Hook (15 sec): *Pragnya*
- Imagine your grandparent lives alone.
- One day, while walking down the stairs, they miss a step and fall. They try to catch themself, but they can’t.
- Now what?
- They're lying at the bottom of the stairs, unable to get up, and no one knows it happened.
- In the United States, more than 43,000 adults aged 65 and older die from falls each year.
## Problem (15 sec): *Pragnya*
- FallGuard is our proposed solution to this unfortunate reality that many older adults face.
- Today, many fall-detection solutions rely on smartphones, cameras, or devices that require the user to manually call for help.
- But what happens when the person is unconscious or unable to reach their phone?
## The Solution (30 sec): *Nidhi*
- FallGuard is a wearable, automatic fall-detection system designed to recognize when a person falls and immediately triggers an alert.
- Our system utilizes a sensor that detects a certain impact, and triggers a loud alarm noise.
- Consequently, this also alerts anyone registered on our app. 
## Who would buy this (15 sec): *Nidhi*
- Our primary customers would be families and caregivers of older adults who live alone.
- Rather than relying on the person to recognize their own emergency, FallGuard provides an additional layer of protection that works automatically.
## The Demo (2 min): *Both*

- First, we show the video
- Then we show app simulation
## Real World Impact (30 sec): *Both (Half-Half)*
- But imagine where this technology could be 20 years from now.
- FallGuard could revolutionize how nursing homes and home-care providers monitor and respond to falls.
- And the technology doesn't have to stop with older adults.
- Anyone who is at a higher risk of falling—from young children and babies to people with mobility challenges—could potentially benefit from this technology.
- Because when a fall happens, the most important question shouldn’t be ‘What now?’—it should be ‘How fast can we get help?
