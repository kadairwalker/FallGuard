    Nothing has happened yet — try simulating a fall above.

    A waist-worn accelerometer feeds a Raspberry Pi at roughly 50 readings a second. When the freefall → impact → stillness signature of a real fall is confirmed, a buzzer sounds immediately — and this notification cascade starts in parallel, checking on the care recipient and pulling in their emergency contact if they can't respond.

    Demo timing: each notification stage waits 8 seconds for a response instead of the minutes a real device would allow. Detection itself is classical signal processing on the real device — no LLM involved.
